Your mentor

Small groups. Personal mentoring. The only way I know to make better pilots.

Miki Mohos

SHV/BAZL instructor no. 54547 · 250+ pilots trained · flying since 2017

I believe there is only one way to become a better pilot: someone who knows you, watches you fly, and builds your next step around you.

I bring teaching experience from the University of Bern and a long interest in psychology. Two pilots can fly the same thermal and live through two completely different flights. So I look at the person first: how you experience a situation, what makes you tense, what gives you confidence.

Empathy is not a soft extra in this sport. It is how fear turns into skill. Your training plan follows your attitude and your pace, not a fixed syllabus.

Good questions

Are there students on these days?

No. The flying days are for licensed pilots only: SHV brevet, IPPI 4 or IPPI 5. That goes for pass holders and friends sent in your place. Students train in the school's own courses.

How big are the groups?

Small enough for personal coaching. On groundhandling, thermal and cross-country days there are max. 6 pilots per coach: from 7 pilots, a second experienced coach joins. On maneuver days you fly one at a time with Miki on the radio, and a launch assistant keeps the turnaround fast. Max. 12 pilots per day: Season Pass holders always have their place, Trial Pass holders fill the rest.

What if the weather takes a day?

That is what the full-payback weather guarantee is for. The lost day moves to the next free backup day: there is a backup day for every coaching day. A day counts as held once we have trained for at least half a day, on the ground or in the air, also if you decide not to launch. If the weather stops us earlier, it counts as half a day and you get half a backup day. No training possible at all? The whole day moves. If even they are used up, you choose per lost day: CHF 220 back in cash (what a flying day costs you in the pass) or a CHF 270 voucher. The voucher is valid for next year's Academy pass or for equipment (glider, harness or reserve at list price) in the next calendar year.

Which topic is on which day?

The weather decides. Over the season we cover all four topics: groundhandling and maneuvers in spring, thermals and cross-country in summer. Miki picks the topic that fits the day and announces it in the group, usually the evening before.

What if I cannot make a day?

Send a licensed friend in your place. If you are ill, you can join any backup day that runs instead. Ill or injured for the rest of the season? Tell Miki before the next flying day and you get a CHF 220 voucher for every flying day still ahead. Other missed days are not refunded, also when a backup day you wanted to join is rained off: the weather guarantee covers the season's flying days, not your own attendance. That is what keeps the pass this affordable. TODO_CONFIRM

Can I cancel the pass?

Until 20 February you get everything back except CHF 200. After that the pass is not refunded, but you can send a friend in your place. TODO_CONFIRM

Do you help with equipment?

Yes. Equipment advice is free if you buy your gear with us, and CHF 200 if you buy elsewhere. Pass holders get an equipment voucher worth CHF 1,050 in their academy year, CHF 750 of it on a new glider.

Can I book online coaching without a pass?

Yes. CHF 60 for 30 minutes, about anything that moves you as a pilot: XC analysis, your flying day, gear, weather or mindset. Mentoring is included in both passes: a 30-minute session once a month.

How do the online evenings work?

They are live and last two hours. One evening costs CHF 60, all 4 evenings CHF 200, and they are included in both passes. TODO_RECORDING

Does a second season make sense?

Yes. The four topics stay, your goals grow: a longer cross-country line, more demanding maneuvers, stronger thermals. Miki sets your personal targets at the start of each season. This year's pass holders also get a priority window to book next year's pass before sales open to everyone.
