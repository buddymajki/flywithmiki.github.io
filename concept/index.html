Spring

Full-payback weather guarantee: you do not lose a flying day to the weather

8 flying days, 8 backup days. A day lost to the weather moves to a backup day. If the weather still wins, you get the full value back per lost day: CHF 220 in cash or a CHF 270 voucher.

The fine print

Backup days: 22 to 23 May, 24 to 25 July, 4 to 5 September, 2 to 3 October. We decide at 18:00 the evening before and post it in the group.

A day counts as held once we have trained for at least half a day, on the ground or in the air, also if you decide not to launch. If the weather stops us earlier, it counts as half a day and you get half a backup day. No training possible at all? The whole day moves.

CHF 220 is what a flying day costs you in the pass, CHF 270 is the price of a single day. The guarantee counts the season's flying days, not your own attendance: every pass holder gets the same per missing day.

Good questions

Are there students on these days?

No. The flying days are for licensed pilots only: SHV brevet, IPPI 4 or IPPI 5. That goes for pass holders, single-day pilots and friends sent in your place. Students train in the school's own courses.

Why only 12 places?

Because the coaching is personal: Miki stays with the group on the radio and on launch, and gives feedback after every flight. With 12 pass holders the groups stay small enough for that, and single places are only sold where pass holders leave room.

What if the weather takes a day?

That is what the full-payback weather guarantee is for. The lost day moves to the next free backup day: there are 8 backup days for 8 flying days. A day counts as held once we have trained for at least half a day, on the ground or in the air, also if you decide not to launch. If the weather stops us earlier, it counts as half a day and you get half a backup day. No training possible at all? The whole day moves. If even they are used up, you choose per lost day: CHF 220 back in cash (what a flying day costs you in the pass) or a CHF 270 voucher (the price of a single day). The voucher is valid for next year's Academy season (pass or single days) or for equipment (glider, harness or reserve at list price) in the next calendar year.

Which topic is on which day?

The weather decides. Over the season we cover all four topics: groundhandling and maneuvers in spring, thermals and cross-country in summer. Miki picks the topic that fits the day and announces it in the group, usually the evening before.

What if I cannot make a day?

Send a licensed friend in your place. If you are ill, you can join any backup day that runs instead. Ill or injured for the rest of the season? Tell Miki before the next flying day and you get a CHF 220 voucher for every flying day still ahead. Other missed days are not refunded, also when a backup day you wanted to join is rained off: the weather guarantee covers the season's flying days, not your own attendance. That is what keeps the pass this affordable. TODO_CONFIRM

Can I cancel the pass?

Until 20 February you get everything back except CHF 200. After that the pass is not refunded, but you can send a friend in your place. TODO_CONFIRM

Can I book single days?

Yes, if places are left. Single flying days cost CHF 270 and open on 20 February, after the pass holders. The topic is the one Miki announces for that day.

Do you help with equipment?

Yes. Equipment advice is free if you buy your gear with us, and CHF 200 if you buy elsewhere. Pass holders get an equipment voucher worth CHF 1,050 in their academy year, CHF 750 of it on a new glider.

How do the online evenings work?

They are live and last two hours. One evening costs CHF 60, all 4 evenings CHF 200, and they are included in the Academy Pass. TODO_RECORDING

Does a second season make sense?

Yes. The four topics stay, your goals grow: a longer cross-country line, more demanding maneuvers, stronger thermals. Miki sets your personal targets at the start of each season. This year's pass holders also get a priority window to book next year's pass before sales open to everyone.
