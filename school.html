🤝 The instructor you will remember in thirty years

Learning to fly is a strangely intimate thing. In your first days you have to trust your instructor the way a child trusts the hand holding the bicycle seat: completely, and before you can know whether that trust is deserved. Those first flights can become some of the most beautiful memories of your life, or some of the worst, and the difference is rarely the weather. It is the person standing next to you.

Read the rest of Miki's letter, and why joining starts with a free video call, not an invoice →

I meet many pilots who arrive frustrated from somewhere else. Not because they were taught wrong things, but because nobody had the patience and empathy they needed at the moment they needed it. Because in the air, what you really meet is not the wind. It is your own limits. With a good mentor beside you, meeting those limits is exactly how you grow into a safe, confident pilot. Without one, it can be how you learn to be afraid.

You can learn paragliding in a factory, twenty students to a hill, next please. It works, on paper. But almost every pilot remembers their first instructor for the rest of their life; the words come back to you at 2,000 meters, thirty years later. I take that responsibility personally.

That is why joining us does not start with an invoice. It starts with a conversation: before you enrol, we sit down for a 30-to-60-minute video call, just the two of us. You learn how I think and how I teach, I learn what you need, and we both find out whether the chemistry is right. At most schools your first contact is a message answered by an administrator, or a short phone call; here it is the person who will stand next to you at your first launch. A course like this is a serious investment, and the most expensive mistake is paying for a school and then discovering you are not on the same wavelength as your instructor, or that there are so many instructors that nobody even knows your name. We rule that out before you pay a single franc.

🪂 Trained for real life, not just for the exam

Some schools train you to pass a test. We train you to fly. That is why our more advanced students start learning the basics of cross-country flying while still in school: Miki sets them simple XC tasks on suitable days, and several students have already completed the roughly 12 km Engelberg-Fürenalp flat triangle before their exam. Our current student record is a 34 km cross-country flight, flown together with Miki. At most schools, a student flying XC before the licence is unthinkable.

The result: when you reach your exam with us, you are not just ready for the exam. You are genuinely ready to make your own decisions in the air, and to launch and land safely even at a site you have never seen before. That is the whole point of a licence.

And the gear keeps up with the ambition: Miki, a PhD engineer, continuously follows what is new on the market and weighs which techniques and equipment genuinely belong in training. The guiding concept: fun and safety with great performance. Modern wings, even in the school class, make 200-300 km flights possible with the right preparation, and two of Miki's own XC records were flown on exactly the wing class you learn on.

📜 The Training, Phase by Phase

Everything below is included in the course price. Click any phase for the full story.

⛰ Training hill (2–5 days) Harness, inflation, kiting, and your very first flights, low and slow +

The training hill is your gateway to soaring through the skies, and those first few days (2-5 days) lay the foundation for your aerial adventures. Here's a glimpse of what to expect:

Harnessing Your Potential: Literally! Getting comfortable in the harness is key. You'll learn how to wear it securely and adjust it for maximum comfort during flight.

Winging It (the Fun Way): Now comes the exciting part - inflating the paraglider! This majestic wing will soon carry you aloft, but first, you'll learn the proper inflation technique to ensure a smooth rise.

Walking on Air (Almost): Ready to feel the lift? We'll progress to ground exercises, also known as kiting. With the wind filling the wing, you'll learn to control it by running and feeling its pull. It's more like a playful dance with the wind than exercise, and tons of fun!

Taking Flight (Low and Slow): The culmination of your training hill experience - your first paragliding flights! These low-altitude flights focus on practicing take-off and landing techniques under the watchful eye of your instructor. You'll experience the thrill of weightlessness and get a taste of the freedom that awaits.

The training hill is where your paragliding journey truly begins. It's a supportive, fun-filled environment where you'll develop the skills and confidence to take to the skies. So, get ready to transform from a curious beginner to a blossoming paraglider pilot! We can't wait to share the magic of flight with you.

🌄 High flights (1–12 months, your pace) Radio-guided flights from the mountains: thermalling, maneuvers, landings. Unlimited, 4–6 per good day +

Once you've mastered the basics, you are ready to take your paragliding adventure to new heights. High altitude flights are where the magic truly unfolds. Get ready for breathtaking views, exhilarating maneuvers, and the art of staying aloft for extended periods. The high flights and theory phase usually takes 1-12 months, depending on your time and weather.

Taking Control We'll refine your glider handling skills, teaching you precise steering techniques that allow you to navigate the vastness of the sky with confidence. This includes mastering turns, banks, and maintaining a steady course - all while enjoying the incredible panorama below.

Understanding Your Wing: High altitude flights provide the perfect opportunity to truly feel the behavior of your paraglider. We'll delve into wing characteristics, teaching you how to read air currents and anticipate how your wing reacts, ensuring you stay comfortably above your head at all times.

Descent with Finesse: Learning to gracefully descend is as important as soaring high. You'll practice maneuvers like big ears and fast circles, gaining control over your descent rate before a smooth and safe landing.

The Art of Thermalling: Imagine using rising air currents to extend your flight time, sometimes for hours on end! That's the magic of thermalling. We'll guide you through identifying thermals, entering them smoothly, and maximizing their lift to keep you soaring effortlessly.

Landing Like a Pro: Soft, precise landings are the hallmark of a skilled paraglider pilot. You'll refine your landing techniques, mastering approach angles, flare control, and ensuring a gentle touchdown every time.

Practice for your exam: As your skills soar, we'll prepare you for the official paragliding exam. You'll practice a designated set of maneuvers that demonstrate your mastery of paragliding techniques. Through focused practice sessions and expert feedback, you'll gain the confidence and competence to ace the exam and officially earn your independent pilot title! The sky will then be your playground, ready for you to explore its endless possibilities.

High altitude flights are about much more than just flying higher. They're about building confidence, mastering control, and unlocking the true potential of paragliding. With expert guidance and practice, you'll transform from a capable pilot to a master of the skies, ready to explore the world from a whole new perspective. Get ready to experience the thrill of extended flights, breathtaking views, and the pure joy of dancing with the wind!

📱 The digital advantage: FlightDeck Your progress, maneuver list and theory in one app, free for our students +

Leave the outdated paper logbooks and scattered notes behind. The Flight Deck is our exclusive digital platform designed to maximize the efficiency and safety of your paragliding education. Unlike traditional methods, this system ensures you are always connected to your progress and your instructor.

See Your Success: The core feature is the personalized dashboard, where you can visually track your personal advancement against training milestones. This transparency is a powerful motivator. You get instant access to a comprehensive maneuver list that complements and enhances the mandatory SHV syllabus. This unique structure ensures maneuvers are taught in a much more logical, sequential building-block order, making the training process smoother and safer.

Instructor Collaboration: The platform allows your instructor to see your real-time progress. This instant transparency is crucial, it enables us to quickly decide together exactly what you need to practice and develop next. By eliminating guesswork and streamlining communication, Flight Deck makes your entire training regimen dramatically more efficient and safer, ensuring every flight is focused and productive.

📖 Theory (5 subjects, included) Meteorology, aerodynamics, flight technique, airspace law and materials. Taught, not just handed to you +

Paragliding isn't just about feeling the wind in your hair. It's about becoming a pilot who understands the science and art of flight. While practice makes the master, a solid foundation in theory is essential for safe and enjoyable flying. That's where theory class comes in!

Weather wisdom Meteorology takes center stage. You'll learn to read weather patterns, understand wind dynamics, clouds, fronts, alpine weather and most importantly, make informed decisions about when to fly and when to stay grounded. Safety is paramount, and knowing how weather conditions impact your flight is crucial.

The Physics of Flight: Aerodynamics demystifies the magic behind your wing. You'll explore the science of lift, drag, and how your glider interacts with the air. This knowledge empowers you to fly with confidence, understanding the forces at play and how to manipulate them for optimal performance.

Perfecting Your Technique: Flight practice theory goes beyond simply taking off and landing. Learn the secrets to smooth take-offs, flawless maneuvers, route-planning and graceful landings. This theoretical understanding, coupled with practical exercises, equips you for a truly polished piloting experience.

Knowing the Rules of the Sky: Paragliding isn't a free-for-all. You'll delve into airspace regulations - legislation, learning where and when you can fly. This ensures the safety of yourself and other airspace users, fostering a harmonious flying environment.

Gear Up with Knowledge: Material science sheds light on the equipment you rely on. You'll gain insights into the properties of your wing, harness, and other gear, ensuring you treat them with proper care and understand their limitations.

Theory lessons may not be as exhilarating as soaring through the skies, but it's the cornerstone of safe and successful paragliding. With a solid foundation in theory, you'll take to the air with confidence, ready to explore the boundless possibilities of flight.
