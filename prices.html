Bringing your own equipment?

Then the training is CHF 6,300 (Basic Training CHF 1,900 + High Flight Course CHF 4,400, whether you take them together or as two separate steps), and it includes a 30-minute review of your equipment: I look at your wing, harness and reserve and tell you honestly whether I consider them airworthy, because I will not send you into the air on gear I have not seen. If the review shows something needs a closer look, I can do a full check and a reserve repack for you at our Checkcenter (full check CHF 300, reserve repack CHF 90), or recommend the right equipment if the honest conclusion is that yours should not fly. My advice either way: talk to me before you buy anything, anywhere. Porous fabric, lines shrunk out of trim, a reserve unpacked for years, a wing outside your weight range: none of it is visible in a photo, and a check costs a fraction of a wrong purchase. Buying used somewhere else? Send me the listing first, for free, even if you end up buying it from someone else.

Transparency, and no fine print

Why the total, and not just our course price?

Because CHF 11,000 to 12,000 is what you will actually spend. You will find schools advertising CHF 2,000 or 3,000; those numbers are real, but they are fragments: a part-course without the training hill, or a price that only applies if you buy your wing there. Wherever you end up in Switzerland, the honest end total lands in the same range. We simply print it first, so you can choose your instructor instead of decoding a price list.

At that price you are buying the most affordable entry into real aviation there is. Your wing lasts for years, the thermals are free, and once you hold the licence a full day of flying costs you a cable-car ticket.
